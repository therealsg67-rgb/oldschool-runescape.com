WHO ARE WE

WE ARE JAGEX: THE RUNESCAPE COMPANY

Based in Cambridge UK, and founded in 2001, we're a global video game developer who creates living, evolving worlds shaped by a passionate community of players all united by shared adventures.

Most importantly, we are the home of RuneScape - one of the most enduring and influential online game universes ever created, welcoming over 300m accounts since launch.

Today, as we celebrate 25 years of gameplay, Jagex: The RuneScape Company is entering a new era - one defined by strong player-led growth, with the worlds of RuneScape more alive, more relevant and more widely played than ever before. All laying the foundations for the boldest chapter in RuneScape’s history.

    JAGEX FOUNDED

    Originally founded in 2000 in Cambridge, UK, by Andrew Gower, with a simple but ambitious idea: to create an online fantasy game that could be played directly in a web browser and evolve continuously alongside its players.

    RUNESCAPE'S BIRTH

    They began developing the MMORPG in 1999 launching the free to play version in Jan 2001 quickly grew into one of the world’s most recognisable and enduring MMORPGs. Built on accessibility, depth, and player freedom, RuneScape pioneered many of the principles that define modern online games, persistent worlds, live updates, and community-led evolution.

RS25

CELEBRATING 25 YEARS OF RUNESCAPE

In 2026, Runescape turned 25. Few games if any achieve that longevity let alone while remaining culturally relevant and actively played. RS25 is a celebration of that achievement and a statement of intent.  

As we look ahead, we’re evolving into Jagex: The Runescape Company, focused on growing and stewarding the Runescape universe across multiple games and experiences, and building it to thrive for decades to come.
