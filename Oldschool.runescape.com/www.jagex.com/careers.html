OUR CULTURE

At Jagex, culture isn’t a buzzword – we live it every day.

At Jagex, culture isn’t a buzzword - it’s how we work. The community spirit that drives our games also drives our studio, shaping a workplace built on openness, respect, and supporting each other.

We invest in people at every career stage through mentorship and learning, and we know great ideas come from diverse perspectives. Curiosity and creative problem-solving are part of our everyday mindset. Kindness, community, and passion for our craft define who we are.

WHERE WE WORK

Our HQ is at One Granta Park, just south of Cambridge city in the UK. Set within one of Europe’s leading science and technology campuses, our office is surrounded by green space and a vibrant innovation community. It’s a place designed for collaboration, creativity and focus.

Around 50 miles from London and within easy reach of Cambridge’s rail links to the rest of the country, the studio is simple to get to. With open team spaces alongside quieter areas for focused work, it supports how game teams really work, and remains a central part of Jagex life within our flexible, hybrid working culture.
