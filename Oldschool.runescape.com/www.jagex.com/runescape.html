RUNESCAPE

Celebrating 25 years, RuneScape is one of the world’s longest-running MMOs—praised by PC Gamer as “one of the most endearing and unique.” Available on PC and mobile, it’s a living world shaped by its community.

Set in the fantasy realm of Gielinor, forge your own path through gods, legends, quests, dungeons, and riches. As we enter the next chapter, we’re investing in ambitious content, stronger trust and integrity, and rewarding players’ time and creativity. With frequent updates and a clear long-term vision, the adventure is far from over.

OLD SCHOOL RUNESCAPE

Old School RuneScape is the definitive retro fantasy MMORPG. Launched in 2013 from a 2007 snapshot, it was revived by community demand—and uniquely governed by player voting, where major changes need player approval.

Blending classic visuals with modern challenge, Old School offers a vast open world of quests, social skilling, competitive modes, and epic co-op. It’s a true forever game: nostalgic in spirit, modern in ambition, and evolving with its empowered global community. Winner of BAFTA Mobile Game of the Year (2019).

RUNESCAPE: DRAGONWILDS

A bold new chapter in the RuneScape franchise,  Dragonwilds is a co-operative survival crafting adventure set on the forgotten continent of Ashenfall, where dragons have awakened. Gather, build, craft, and wield powerful magic as you uncover ancient secrets and fight to survive, solo or with up to three allies. Powered by Unreal Engine 5, and passing 1m copies sold on Steam just 8 months after launch to early access, , Dragonwilds blends high fantasy, light RPG systems, and iconic RuneScape lore in a bold new chapter for the RuneScape universe.

CELEBRATING 25 YEARS OF RUNESCAPE

In 2026, Runescape turns 25. Few games if any achieve that longevity let alone while remaining culturally relevant and actively played. RS25 is a celebration of that achievement, but also a statement of intent.  

As we look ahead, we’re evolving into Jagex: The Runescape Company, focused on growing and stewarding the Runescape universe across multiple games and experiences, and building it to thrive for decades to come.
