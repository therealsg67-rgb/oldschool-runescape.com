Name Release Begins Today!

05 November 2026
Update - 05/11 | 14:15 UTC

Since it has been a couple of hours since our first batch release, we wanted to share a quick update addressing some of your concerns and give you some extra clarity on what's happened so far.

We've been keeping a close eye on who has been taking the names from our first batch. So far, 72% of names have gone to players with >1800 total level on OSRS or >2000 total level on RuneScape, with more than 60% of names going to players with >100 days of play time and a further ~30% to players with at least 30 days of play time. These stats give us confidence these names are going out to real players.

Issues & Actions

In regards to the error "Unable to connect: Unexpected server response", we've made some changes which have eased these errors. The team will be keeping a close eye on this throughout today and throughout the future batch releases.

The team are also aware that some players are running into issues trying to set a name during Tutorial Island which is currently being investigated.

Clarifications

Any exploits that have been mentioned, including using offensive or vulgar names to bypass the checks we've put in place do not work. Any forced name changes are not validated with a skill total check and therefore can only claim names that would have been available regardless, not names that are part of this release.

Some players may have also seen names not beginning with the letter 'A' being claimed today. With the new improvements to the in-game system, this means that it can now correctly pick up names from our previous name release in 2017. These names could have been claimed through the website before our improvements on Tuesday, but for whatever reason were not.

Though imperfect, the new systems we've put in place for this name release have helped us in preventing and helping identify bad actors and further bans are being issued, in addition to the 11k we previously announced. We will continue to monitor all of our systems throughout this name release and make adjustments accordingly.

Today marks the first day of name batches being released for this year's Name Release! This release contains approximately ~38 million names.

This newspost is to give you a breakdown of the upcoming batches and what dates we're aiming to release them. We'll look to put each batch out at 11:00 UTC every weekday until all batches have been released.

These names are taken from abandoned characters which must have been inactive for at least 2 years AND either been permanently banned, has never been a member or membership was only paid in bonds, which ended at least 5 years ago.

To claim a name, you must now be a RuneScape member, with a skill total of at least 1200. You can claim a name via the in-game 'Check Names' function or through our website.

Extra Measures

For this Name Release, we wanted to take learnings from the previous one in 2017. As of now, we've already banned over 11,000 characters we believe are going to be used for name sniping and are in the process of acting against more. The anti-cheat team are going to be paying close attention during the full duration of this name release for bad actors and unusual behaviour that would indicate characters being used for malicious intent.

On the web side, we've made several integral changes to help ensure we can also prevent and take action against any bad actors. We won't be disclosing any specifics here to not give any indication to those with poor intentions, but all teams involved are going to be very reactive in response to any malicious behaviour.

You'll also notice a queuing system both in-game and on the website that gives us the flexibility to increase or decrease wait time depending on traffic and unusual indications to help facilitate any further actions we may take against those acting in bad faith and protect legitimate players. Where any bad actors are detected, we'll reserve the right to release those names again.

Additional Information

Remember: your previous name will be reserved to your character for at least 35 days, or for up to 6 months, depending on how long you had it. You'll lose this reserved name if you change your name again, the name is removed from the game, or the reservation period ends.

If you've changed your name recently, you'll have a 28 day cooldown which can be removed by redeeming a Bond. If you have any questions or require some help, visit our Support Centre. The team cannot help with getting players a particular name nor looking up whether a name would be included within a certain batch.

Something to bear in mind for any players creating characters for Hardcore Worlds, these won't be eligible until you've reached the requirements listed above. If your character resets, these won't be eligible unless the character has the required skill total, as described above.
